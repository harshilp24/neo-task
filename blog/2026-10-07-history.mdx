History lists every task you've viewed or acted on in the last 30 days, so you can return to one without searching.

History is for anyone who works on tasks in more than one project. It needs no setup and starts recording from this release, so it's empty on your first visit.

- Open the **History** tab to see your tasks under **Today**, **Past Week** and **Past Month**.
- Click a row to open the task. A completed or discarded task can be reopened from there.
