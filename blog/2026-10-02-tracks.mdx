A task in Tasket can now move through several workstreams at once, each with its own status. Turn on **Tracks** for a project, add a track for each discipline, and every team sees the task in its own column.

{/* truncate */}

## What's new

- **Tracks board.** A new **Tracks** tab shows one column per track, each split into *In Progress* and *Done*. A task on three tracks appears in all three columns.
- **Separate status per track.** **Start** a task on a track, **Mark Done** when that workstream finishes, and **Mark Pending** if it needs more work. Each track's status is independent, and none of them completes the task.
- **Tracks pill on every task.** Open any task, from the board, **My Work** or **Updates**, to see its status on every track and change it in place.
- **Start in bulk.** **Select** tasks in one column and start them all on other tracks in one go.
- **Works offline.** **Start**, **Mark Done** and **Mark Pending** sync when you reconnect.

## Good to know

- Any team member, guests included, can turn on Tracks and create tracks. Only admins can turn Tracks off.
- **Deleting a track is permanent.** It removes that track's statuses from every task, including closed ones.
- **Turning Tracks off clears the project's tracks.** Turning it back on starts with an empty board.
- Moving a task to another project clears its tracks, even if the new project has tracks with the same names.
- Completed and discarded tasks leave the board but keep their track statuses. Reopen one and it returns where it was.
