# Member

A person from your organisation who belongs to a team in Tasket.

## Who they are

- Does the day-to-day work: creates tasks, comments, attaches Studio docs, chats with Friday.
- Usually the person setting up a feature for their own project.
- Reads tutorials once and how-to guides often.

## What they can do

Take this from the feature's PRD permissions table. If a feature doesn't say, log a question; don't assume members can do everything.

For Tracks, a member can turn Tracks on, add, rename, move and delete tracks, and change a task's status on any track. A member can't turn Tracks off.

## How to write for them

- Assume they know Tasket basics (teams, projects, tasks) and nothing about the new feature.
- In **Before you begin**, say what they need in their own terms: "Access to the project", not "Project-level member permission".
- When a step needs an admin, say so in the step's section, before the steps: "Only admins can turn Tracks off." Then send them to an admin rather than leaving them stuck.
