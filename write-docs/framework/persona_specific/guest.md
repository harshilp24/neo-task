# Guest

A person from another organisation who's been added to a team.

## Who they are

- Works with your team on some projects, such as an agency or a contractor.
- Sees only the projects they've been given access to.
- May be new to Tasket itself, not just to the feature.

## What they can do

Guest permissions vary by feature, so always check the PRD. For Tracks, the PRD says guests have the same track permissions as members on projects they can access. That includes deleting a track, which can't be undone; log it as a question to confirm it's intended.

## How to write for them

- Say explicitly whether guests can do something. Don't let "any team member" leave it unclear.
- In the reference permissions grid, give guests their own column.
- Avoid assuming knowledge of your organisation's setup; link to the Tasket overview where it helps.
