# Admin

A member of a team who also manages it.

## Who they are

- Can do everything a member can, plus team-level actions the PRD reserves for admins.
- Often the person asked to undo or approve something a member couldn't do.
- Reads the reference and the destructive sections of guides closely.

## What they can do

Take this from the PRD. For Tracks, the only admin-only action is turning Tracks off for a project.

## How to write for them

- Put admin-only actions in their own section, starting with "Only admins can…".
- Admin-only actions are often destructive. Give the warning before the steps, and say what's lost and whether it can be recovered.
- In the reference permissions grid, show the admin column even when it matches members, so the difference is visible at a glance.
