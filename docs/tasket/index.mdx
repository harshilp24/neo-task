**Tasket** is where your team plans and runs its work in Neo. Each task carries its own context, the discussion, the Studio docs and the Friday chat, so anyone who picks it up, Friday included, has what they need.

## How Tasket is organised

- **Teams.** The people who work together. A team has members and admins from your organisation, and guests from other organisations.
- **Projects.** Each team keeps its work in projects. A project holds a set of tasks.
- **Tasks.** One unit of work, in exactly one project. A task has a title, a comment thread, attached Studio docs and a Friday chat. It has one creator, up to ten assignees and any number of watchers.

## Task status

A task is **Open**, **Completed** or **Discarded**. Completed and discarded tasks are closed. You can reopen a closed task, which makes it Open again.

## Where your work shows up

- **Work tab.** Every task you're assigned to, across your projects.
- **Updates tab.** Activity on the tasks you take part in.

## Tracks

Some tasks pass through several teams at once, such as spec, design and QA. Tracks give a task a separate status in each of those workstreams.
