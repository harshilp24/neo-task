**History** is your personal list of every task you've viewed or acted on in the last 30 days, with the most recent first. It works like the history in a browser, limited to Tasket. Use it to return to a task without searching for it.

- **It updates itself.** History needs no action from you. Tasket adds a task whenever you view it or act on it, from any view.
- **It covers every project.** History lists tasks from all your projects, so each row shows the project's name.
- **It's yours.** History records only your own activity, and it follows you across your devices.

This page explains why History exists, how it works, and when to use it. It has no steps. To use History, follow [Return to recent tasks](./return-to-recent-tasks.mdx).

## Why search isn't enough

You often need a task again shortly after you leave it. Ask *"Where's the task I had open this morning?"* and the usual answer is to search for it.

History is the quickest way back, with no search. The task you opened this morning is under **Today**, whichever project it belongs to and whichever view you opened it from.

## How History works

**History updates every time you open a task or act on it, from anywhere in Tasket.**

Opening a task's detail view adds the task to History. So does acting on it: commenting, reassigning, marking it as reviewed, completing, discarding or watching it. Hovering over a task's row doesn't count.

History groups tasks into three sections by your most recent access:

- **Today** holds the tasks you've used since midnight.
- **Past Week** holds the seven days before today.
- **Past Month** holds the days before that, back to 30 days.

A task appears once. If you open a task from **Past Month**, it moves to the top of **Today**. Days start at midnight in your local timezone, so a task you view at 11:30 PM counts as today.

Each row shows the task as it is now, including its status and silence score. A task that someone else completed or discarded since your last visit stays in History with its new status, and you can reopen it from there.

:::note[History starts on the day the feature is released]

Tasks you used before the release aren't included. Your first visit shows an empty History, and it fills as you work.

:::

## How History differs from the Work tab

Both list tasks, but they answer different questions. The **Updates** tab is different again: it lists activity on tasks you take part in.

| | Work tab | History |
|---|---|---|
| Lists | tasks assigned to you | tasks you've viewed or acted on in the last 30 days |
| Project name on each row | not shown | shown |
| A task appears because | someone assigned it to you | you opened it or acted on it |

## When to use History

Use History to get back to a task you opened recently, when you remember roughly when you used it. It also keeps completed and discarded tasks in reach, which is useful when one needs reopening.

Skip History for tasks you haven't opened in the last 30 days, or have never opened. They aren't in it.

## What to know about History

Three rules shape what you find in History:

- **Tasks leave after 30 days.** A task you last used more than 30 days ago is no longer listed. Opening it again brings it back.
- **Deleted tasks disappear.** History removes a deleted task's row and shows no marker for it.
- **Losing access doesn't remove a task.** A task you can no longer edit stays in History and opens read-only.

The full rules are in the [History reference](./reference.mdx).
