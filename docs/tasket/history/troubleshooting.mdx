This page covers the most common problems with History in Tasket: errors, symptoms such as an empty list or a missing task, offline and permission limits, and data loss. Each one shows what you see, its cause and the solution.

For steps, see [Return to recent tasks](./return-to-recent-tasks.mdx). For how History behaves in each case, see the [History reference](./reference.mdx).

## History doesn't load

:::danger[Error]

The **History** tab shows a full-page error instead of your tasks.

:::

### Cause

Tasket couldn't load your History.

### Solution

Use the retry option on the error page.

## Task no longer exists

:::danger[Error]

You click a row in History, and a message says the task no longer exists. The row then disappears.

:::

### Cause

The task was deleted, and History hadn't yet removed its row.

### Solution

Nothing brings a deleted task back into History.

## Task can't be reopened

:::danger[Error]

You click **Re-open**, and an error says the task couldn't be reopened.

:::

### Cause

A network error stopped the change. The task's status hasn't changed.

### Solution

Check your connection, then click **Re-open** again.

## History is empty

:::info[Symptom]

The **History** tab shows no tasks.

:::

### Cause

One of these applies:

- You haven't viewed or acted on a task since History was released. Activity from before the release isn't included.
- Every task you used is older than 30 days.

### Solution

Open a task from any view. It appears under **Today**.

## Task missing from History

:::info[Symptom]

A task you remember using isn't in any section.

:::

### Cause

One of these applies:

- You last viewed or acted on the task more than 30 days ago.
- The task was deleted. History removes deleted tasks and shows no marker for them.
- You hovered over the task's row without opening it. Hovering doesn't add a task to History.

### Solution

If the task still exists, open it from its project. It returns to History under **Today**.

## Task in the wrong section

:::info[Symptom]

A task you used today sits under **Past Week** or **Past Month**, or the other way round.

:::

### Cause

You viewed the task offline. While you're offline, History sorts tasks by your device's clock. A clock that's wrong by more than 24 hours, or that passed midnight while you were offline, puts the task in the wrong section.

### Solution

Reconnect. Tasket corrects the section when the access syncs.

## Sections don't stay collapsed

:::info[Symptom]

A section you collapsed is expanded again, or **Today** is collapsed when you open History.

:::

### Cause

Collapsed sections aren't remembered between sessions. Each new session starts with every section expanded. **Today** is the exception: it starts collapsed whenever it has no tasks.

### Solution

Collapse or expand the section again. To fill **Today**, open a task.

## History needs a connection

:::caution[Offline]

History shows "History requires an internet connection for your first load."

:::

### Cause

You're offline, and this device has no saved copy of your History yet.

### Solution

Reconnect and open the **History** tab. After the first load, History also opens offline.

## History looks out of date

:::caution[Offline]

History shows an offline banner, and recent activity from your other devices is missing.

:::

### Cause

You're offline, so History shows the copy saved on this device.

### Solution

Reconnect. History updates.

## Task opens read-only

:::caution[Permission]

A task opened from History shows a notice that you no longer have edit access. You can't assign, comment, complete, discard or watch.

:::

### Cause

You were removed from the task's assignees and watchers, or you no longer belong to the task's workspace. History keeps the row, but the task opens read-only.

### Solution

You can still read the task. To act on it, you need access to the task again.

## Reopened task is closed again

:::danger[Data loss]

A task you reopened offline goes back to *Completed* or *Discarded* after you reconnect, and an error appears.

:::

### Cause

The reopen couldn't sync, so Tasket put the task back to the status it has on the server.

### Solution

Open the task and click **Re-open** again while you're connected.

## Offline visit missing from History

:::danger[Data loss]

A task you opened offline was in **Today** on that device, but it isn't in History on your other devices.

:::

### Cause

The record of that visit never synced. The task itself isn't affected.

### Solution

Open the task again while you're connected. It appears under **Today** on every device.
