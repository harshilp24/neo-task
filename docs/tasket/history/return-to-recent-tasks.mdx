[History](./what-is-history.mdx) lists every task you've viewed or acted on in the last 30 days, from all your projects, with the most recent first.

In this guide, you open a recent task from History, collapse the sections you don't need, reopen a completed or discarded task, and use History offline.

## Before you begin

Make sure you have:

- **A task you've already opened.** History fills itself as you view and act on tasks. It needs no setup.
- **A connection for the first load.** After that, History also opens offline.

## Open a recent task

1. In Tasket, open the **History** tab.

   Your tasks are grouped under **Today**, **Past Week** and **Past Month**, with the most recent first in each section.

2. Find the task in the section that matches when you last used it.

   Each row shows the task's title, ID, project name and current status.

3. Click the task's row.

   The task opens in its detail view, the same as from any other view. In History, it moves to the top of **Today**.

## Collapse a section

To focus on one time period:

1. On the **History** tab, collapse the section you don't need from its header.

   The section's tasks are hidden. Its header stays, with an expand icon.

2. To show the tasks again, click the expand icon.

Tasket doesn't remember collapsed sections. The next session starts with every section expanded, except **Today**, which starts collapsed when it has no tasks.

## Reopen a completed or discarded task

Any participant on the task can reopen it: its creator, an assignee, a contributor or a watcher.

1. On the **History** tab, click the completed or discarded task.

   The row shows the task's status, so you can tell which tasks are closed.

2. In the task's detail view, find **Re-open**. To add context, enter a comment in the text field below it.

   The comment is optional.

3. Click **Re-open**.

   The task's status changes to *Open*. The task returns to the project's **Open** tab and to the assignee's **Work** tab, and it stays in your History.

## Use History offline

History opens offline from the copy saved on your device, with an offline banner. The list may be out of date until you reconnect.

- A task you open offline goes to the top of **Today** and syncs when you reconnect.
- A task you reopen offline shows as *Open* on your device. If the sync fails, its status goes back and an error appears.
