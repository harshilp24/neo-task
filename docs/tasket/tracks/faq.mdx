Tracks is the Tasket feature that gives one task a separate status on each workstream, such as Spec, Design and QA. Here are the short answers:

- **Add a track between two others:** click the **+** between the two columns.
- **Rename, reorder or delete a track:** click the arrow next to the track's name, then **Rename**, **Move track** or **Delete**.
- **Turn off Tracks:** an admin turns it off in the project's **Settings** tab.
- **What can't be undone:** deleting a track and turning Tracks off. Both remove track statuses from every task.
- **Connection:** all of these changes need one.

The steps for each are below. If something isn't working, see [Troubleshoot Tracks](./troubleshooting.mdx).

## How do I add a track between two others?

To add a track between two others in Tasket, click the **+** between those columns on the **Tracks** board, then name the track.

The new column appears in that position, and the columns after it are renumbered. **+ Add Track** after the last column adds a track at the end.

## How do I rename a track?

To rename a track in Tasket, click **Rename** in the track's menu on the **Tracks** board.

1. Click the arrow next to the track's name.
2. Click **Rename** and enter the new name.

   The new name shows on the column and in every task's Tracks pill. Statuses on the track don't change.

## How do I reorder tracks?

To reorder tracks in Tasket, click **Move track** in the track's menu on the **Tracks** board.

1. Click the arrow next to the track's name.
2. Click **Move track**, then choose the position you want.

   The columns are renumbered in their new order. No task changes.

## How do I delete a track?

To delete a track in Tasket, click **Delete** in the track's menu on the **Tracks** board.

:::warning[Deleting a track can't be undone]

It removes the track and every task's status on it, closed tasks included. To change the board's layout instead, rename or reorder the track.

:::

1. Click the arrow next to the track's name.
2. Click **Delete**.

   The column disappears from the board and from every Tracks pill.

## How do I turn off Tracks?

To turn off Tracks in Tasket, an admin turns off **Tracks** in the project's **Settings** tab.

:::warning[Turning Tracks off clears every track]

It removes every track and every task's status on them. If you turn Tracks back on later, the board starts empty.

:::

1. Open the project and click the **Settings** tab.
2. Turn off **Tracks**.

   The **Tracks** tab disappears, and tasks no longer show a Tracks pill.
